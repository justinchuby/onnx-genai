fix(pipeline): a composite workflow reports why it actually stopped

B2 checkpoint C, first half: the real `finish_reason` the convergence report
flagged as fabricated.

`WorkflowRuntime::generate_with_callbacks` returned `FinishReason::MaxTokens`
unconditionally — a constant, not an observation. Every composite workflow
generation claimed it had run out of budget, including runs the workflow ended
itself. That is the single distinction a finish reason exists to make: a client
deciding whether to continue a truncated response would have continued one the
model considered complete, and nothing in the result would have told it
otherwise.

The reason is only knowable inside the loop, because the loop is what decided.
`WorkflowRunTelemetry::loop_ended_by_predicate` records whether the outermost
generation loop stopped because its liveness predicate went false — the model's
own stop — or because it exhausted its bound. The counters carry it out, and
`generate_with_callbacks` reports it, for the streamed per-token reason as well
as the final result.

**Also fixed: a committed fixture that could not execute.**
`tiny-llm-scatter-workflow` declared its cache ports as
`[batch, cache_capacity, 4]` while its ONNX graph exposes `[batch, 16, 16]`, so
loading it for generation failed on the contract mismatch. The workflow now
states the graph's real extent. Its existing suite
(`workflow_derived_static_cache`, 4 cases) still passes, and the fixture gets
further along than before — it now reaches a *second* pre-existing drift
(`next_accepted_len` declared as an output the graph does not expose) that I
have deliberately not chased here.

**What the new test asserts, and what it does not.** The bound case is asserted
end-to-end. The predicate case is not, because no committed composite fixture
both executes through `generate` and stops on its own liveness predicate: the
authored `decoder` fixture always runs to its bound, `gemma4_chained` is driven
through the proposal API, and `tiny-llm-scatter-workflow` has the drift above.
Asserting it against a fixture that cannot reach the state would be a test
passing for the wrong reason, so the gap is written into the test rather than
papered over, and the mechanism is covered at unit level.
