test(fixtures): raise this branch's ONNX fixtures to IR 11 / opset 24 and guard it

Repository requirement: a new or modified test fixture ONNX model must declare
IR >= 11 and a default-domain opset >= 24.

Audit of everything #1723 adds or copies:

* `gemma4_chained` and `gemma4_chained_mixed` (4 `*.onnx.textproto`) were already
  IR 11 / opset 24, as are the two generators the graphs came from
  (`build_tiny_gemma4_assistant.py`, `build_tiny_gemma4_assistant_mixed.py`), so
  the committed copies and their generators agree -- nothing to regenerate.
* The three models authored inline in `native_workflow_parity.rs` were IR 8 with
  opset 21/13/13. Raised to IR 11 / opset 24. `ArgMax` (since-13), `Add`
  (since-14), and `Less` (since-13) had no behavior change after those versions,
  so this is a version statement rather than a semantic one -- and the parity the
  cases assert is unchanged, verified on ORT, native CPU, and H200 native CUDA
  (`native_workflow_parity` 10/10 and 12/12).

No other ONNX artifact is added by this branch; `input_embedding.f32` and
`tokenizer.json` are not models.

`fixture_ir_opset_floor` is the local guard. It checks the committed packages and
the inline models this PR owns, because inline models are exactly where an old
version creeps back unnoticed -- they are string literals, so no ONNX tool sees
them until a test runs.

Its scope is deliberately narrow. Widening it to pre-existing fixtures would make
this branch red for the mainline fixture-upgrade PR's work, which is the fastest
way to get a guard disabled; the module says so and says what to replace when
that PR lands. The checks themselves already generalize.

The guard is verified two ways rather than trusted: a self-test pins that the
parser handles single-line and block `opset_import` forms, treats a missing
`domain` as the default domain, refuses to let a custom domain satisfy the
default-domain floor, and reports every import so one stale block cannot hide
behind a current one; and reintroducing `ir_version: 8` into
`native_workflow_parity.rs` was confirmed to fail it, naming the offending model.

Verified: `onnx-genai-engine` full suite under `native-backend` -- 70 targets,
zero failures; H200 `native_workflow_parity` 12/12; `cargo fmt --all --check`;
`cargo clippy --locked --all-targets -D warnings`.
