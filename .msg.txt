test(cli): match the stats line shape instead of blocklisting substrings

The stdout-contamination check asserted on the substrings "tok/s" and
"ttft". A model can legitimately generate either as prose, so the test
could fail for a reason unrelated to what it guards -- the same flavour of
mistake as the help test fixed earlier today, which compared merged
stdout+stderr including timestamps and was flaky by construction.

Replaces the blocklist with a shape match for the bracketed compact stats
line: numeric in/out, backend, numeric tok/s, and numeric ttft in ms.

The equality check against --no-stats is kept alongside it. Equality is
what actually guarantees piped stdout is byte-stable and cannot miss a
contaminant nobody thought of; the shape match makes a failure point
directly at stats leakage rather than at unspecified drift. Together they
give completeness and a precise diagnosis, which is why both are worth
having.
