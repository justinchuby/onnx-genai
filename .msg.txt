docs(testing): correct the fixture inventory against what is on disk

Copilot's review flagged the Tier 0 fixture list as stale. Checking it
turned up a mixed result rather than a straightforward one: tiny-llm is
indeed textproto-only and not the .onnx the document implied, but the
tiny-native-* directories do exist, contrary to the review.

Both claims were worth checking rather than accepting. The document now
lists every tests/fixtures entry with its actual format, notes that no
crate-local fixture directories exist outside that tree, and cites only
references that were confirmed -- the env-var-gated real-model tests,
repl_e2e.rs, the cli-ort lane, and the bench binaries.

This matters more here than in most documents because the design is meant
to be executed against in waves. Someone picking up Phase 1 starts from
the fixture list, and pointing them at a fixture that does not exist, or
whose format they assume wrongly, wastes exactly the time the design is
meant to save.

Phase 1 remains achievable; the tiny reasoning fixture it depends on was
already listed as work to create rather than as something already present.
