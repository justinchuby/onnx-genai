The queue depth and KV block utilization tiles above are the entire thesis of this design, side by side. (Named by label, never by position: this caption previously said "tiles two and three" and went on pointing confidently at whichever tiles happened to occupy those slots after one of them was replaced.) 0 is a real measurement — the server was asked, it answered, and the answer was zero. We say so at full contrast, because a measured zero is a fact. — is an absence — this endpoint does not carry KV utilization by contract, and rendering the documented zero it returns would be a silent lie. Same glyph shape, opposite meanings; they must never be confused.

Why this caption is now shorter than the page it describes. Until this commit the paragraph above described a prefix hit rate tile as "a real measurement — the prefix cache genuinely did not hit", while the tile itself carried a tooltip reading "this is a fabricated zero, not a measurement". Both statements were served to visitors, ten lines apart, for the life of the file. The tile had been correctly flipped from a confident 0.0 % to an unavailable —; the caption that pointed at it was not flipped with it. The demonstration and the doctrine drifted apart, and the doctrine was the thing that kept insisting it was right. The tile is gone (the field was cut at the registry), the contrast is restored with a metric whose zero is genuinely measured, and the caption now names its subjects rather than counting to them.
