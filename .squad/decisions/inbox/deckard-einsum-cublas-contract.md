### 2026-09-03: CUDA GEMM plans retain pointer and reduction proofs
**By:** Deckard
**What:** cuBLASLt planning now derives A/B/C/D alignment from the typed tensor origins used by the launch, retains the selected algorithm's queried alignment/split-K/reduction contract, and reuses it only when later pointers satisfy that proof. F16/BF16 heuristics exclude in-place/output-type reductions; unsupported Einsum fast paths fall back to GenericNative. One transactional arithmetic snapshot owns the selected Auto route and its complete resource set, so capture reuses the warmed direct-or-semantic choice and validates every private workspace owner before launch. Integer generic kernels use explicitly unsigned intermediates and narrow at every declared-width operation.
**Why:** Pointer alignment, reduction precision, route choice, and private-resource lifetime are execution preconditions, not shape assumptions. Keeping them in one published snapshot makes warmup, capture, rewarm, and fallback agree while preserving exact modular integer semantics and F32 narrow-once floating-point semantics.
