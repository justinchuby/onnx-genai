### 2026-07-28: Place the PEFT loader in the engine and preserve adapter precision
**By:** Cohaagen
**What:** The self-contained PEFT loader lives in `onnx-genai-engine::lora::format` and emits `onnx_runtime_ir::TensorData`. It preserves fp16 or fp32 input precision and stores contiguous `A_t[K, rank]` and `B_t[rank, N]`. Standard PEFT factors are transposed at load; `fan_in_fan_out=true` also accepts the already-oriented layout described by the design and records which source layout was found.
**Why:** The engine is the design-specified owner and already depends on the runtime tensor representation, so a new crate would add workspace and API overhead without removing a heavy dependency. Preserving the source floating-point dtype avoids an unrequested precision conversion. Current HuggingFace PEFT constructs `lora_A` and `lora_B` as linear weights `[rank, K]` and `[N, rank]` even when `fan_in_fan_out=true`; accepting both that real on-disk form and the design's pre-oriented form keeps the loader compatible while guaranteeing the executor-facing orientation.
