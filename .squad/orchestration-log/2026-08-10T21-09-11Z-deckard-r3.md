**Why:** Device descriptor corruption after ~6 register/Run/unregister cycles; Nabil locked out of factory.rs

## Outcome

Root cause: two-factor bug in `factory.rs`. Factor 1: use-after-free — `ReleaseMemoryInfo` called after `AddAllocatorInfo`; ORT stores raw ptr (doesn't copy). Factor 2: legacy `CreateCpuMemoryInfo` doesn't populate `OrtMemoryInfoDeviceType`/`OrtDeviceMemoryType` → garbage DeviceType:64. Fix: `CreateMemoryInfo_V2` + do not release on success. `conformance_multiple_run_calls` and `ort_loads_our_ep_and_runs_model` pass. Clippy: `manual_dangling_ptr` lint fixed (replaced `1usize as *mut` with `std::ptr::dangling_mut()`). Decision: `deckard-ep-device-lifetime.md`.
