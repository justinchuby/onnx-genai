#2084 has the child report `restricted=` and the parent require it (fail under `NXRT_REQUIRE_PLACEMENT_TESTS=1`, skip with a stated cause otherwise), keeps `allowed == cores` with a corrected comment — it still catches a leader set that isn't one-per-core *on a host that answered* — and replaces the two inline `cfg!(target_os = "linux")` spellings with one `PROCESS_AFFINITY_MASKING_SUPPORTED` constant that is asserted against the implementation that actually compiled.

The general rule I'd take from both PRs: **a premise must be reported by whoever established it, never inferred from a consequence.** Any inference asserts that no failure mode of the premise reproduces that consequence — and here one reproduced it exactly.
